## 2. Closest Prior Literature Overlap Audit

1. **EnergyBench (Genov, Alonso & Bergmeir 2026, [`arXiv:2611.07784`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07784)):** Live gate-aligned benchmark at 11:30 CET/CEST for day-ahead continental markets (BE, NL, FR, DE-LU, DK1, DK2) freezing a single information set; finds grid operators' own pre-gate point forecasts remain strongest on wind/solar. Does not test whether intraday vintage-to-vintage signed ramp timing displacement improves GB outturn ramp warnings over operator trajectories.
2. **Kolkmann, Ostmeier & Weber (2024, *Energy Economics* 139:107890, [`S014098832400598X`](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S014098832400598X)):** Models multivariate continuous intraday wind forecast update processes via copulas on French TSO data for trading/storage simulation. Models joint level-update distributions rather than testing whether an explicit signed steepest-ramp timing displacement improves binary ramp-warning calibration beyond full two-vintage trajectories and pointwise updates.
3. **Meng et al. (*Meteorological Applications*, [`10.1002/met.70203`](https://doi.org/10.1002/met.70203)):** Introduces the Ramp Alignment Score (RAS) to evaluate forecast-vs-realized wind ramp timing errors (Belgian offshore NWP with wind farm parameterization). Evaluates ex-post forecast-vs-realized alignment rather than using pre-cutoff forecast-to-forecast timing displacement as a causal predictor.
4. **Marchal, Bouttier & Nuissier (2025, *NHESS* 25:2613–2628, [`nhess-25-2613-2025`](https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/25/2613/2025/#section5)):** Shows run-to-run risk persistence improves probabilistic warning reliability beyond single runs and lagged ensembles, explicitly noting in §5 that their persistence count does not encode chronological run-to-run trend/timing displacement. **Direct implication:** Our baseline MUST include two-version threshold agreement (risk persistence) AND a lagged ensemble; advancing is justified ONLY by incremental pre-cutoff signed time-shift evidence beyond all of those baselines.
