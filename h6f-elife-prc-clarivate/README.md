## Scientific Summary (`v2` Referee-Revised)

Exploiting the complete `4,551`-paper *eLife* Publish-Review-Curate (PRC) census (`2023–2026`, `4,541 / 4,551` [`99.78%`] true `v1` recovery), live *eLife* article-level `page-views` and `downloads` (`100.00%` of `N = 2,653` mature `2023–2024` `v1` cohorts with strict rolling `2023-11` monthly left-truncation guards), and a strictly audited `30`-journal (`35`-journal expanded) clean life-science OpenAlex panel (`2021H1–2026H1`), this repository replicates four main results:

1. **Voluntary Revision Funnel & Unconditional ESCI ITT / Competing-Risks Survival (Pillar 1):** Across `964` mature `2023–2024` `v1` *Incomplete/Inadequate* preprints, `793` (`82.3%`) converted to VoR and `644` upgraded their *Strength of Evidence* (**`66.8%` of all `964` `v1` *Incomplete* preprints; `81.2%` of the `793` converted VoRs**; `621` crossing to *Solid+*: `64.4%` of all `v1` / `78.3%` of VoRs) across a mean `325.4`-day calendar-date interval between `v1` posting and VoR publication. Aligning Clarivate's ESCI *Solid*-threshold rule cleanly to `v1_dt >= 2024-12-19` (excluding the Oct 1–Dec 18, 2024 transition donut; sensitivities at `>= 2025-01-01` and `>= 2024-10-24`) and estimating **unconditional ITT across all `v1` *Incomplete* preprints** (removing VoR collider conditioning), fixed-horizon VoR conversion fell (`-7.35` pp at `365d`, `p_WCB = 0.009`; `-6.17` pp at `450d`, `p_WCB = 0.015`), unconditional *Solid+* upgrades did not surge (`-3.58` pp at `365d`, `p_WCB = 0.212`; `-3.75` pp at `450d`, `p_WCB = 0.202`), and Aalen–Johansen competing-risks CIF shows a `-5.03` pp drop in unrevised `<Solid` VoRs at `450d` (`14.0% -> 9.0%`, `p = 0.0073`) with *Solid+* CIF converging by `540d` (`54.4%` vs. `54.4%`). Because single-journal before-after ESCI comparisons lack an unexposed control group, we do not claim they prove zero indexing-incentive effects.
2. **Double Evaluation-Market Paradox & `VoR`-Date Autopsy (Pillar 2):** True `v1`-date alignment exposes a `-14.5%` post-`v1` OpenAlex citation penalty for `v1` *Incomplete* ($t = -5.11$, $p = 8.67 \times 10^{-5}$; erased to `+0.7%`, $p = 0.915$ under flawed VoR-date alignment), whereas live readers initially click headline `v1` *Significance* (`+10.0%` views, $p = 0.00077$; `+9.6%` downloads, $p = 0.00217$). Once rolling `2023-11` API left-truncation is eliminated, upgrading *Incomplete* $\to$ *Solid+* yields **zero or at most modest measurable payoff** in post-`v1` citations (`+3.2%`, 95% CI `[-10.7%, +19.2%]`, $p = 0.654$; TOST $\pm 20\%$ $p = 0.0125$, $\pm 15\%$ $p = 0.0508$ just above `0.05`) or post-VoR readership (untruncated `v1 >= 2023-11` rate DiD `+0.058`, $p_{\text{WCB}} = 0.256$, TOST $\pm 15\%$ $p = 0.0426$; clean local VoR event study `N = 651` uniform month-0 spike `+1.11` vs. `+1.11`, FE DiD `+0.001`, $p_{\text{WCB}} = 0.984$; post-VoR `t = +1..+6` `-0.103`, $p_{\text{WCB}} = 0.171$); reader traffic responds instead to **version-release events** (`+0.234` to `+0.301` per published version, $p \le 0.012$).
3. **Submissions vs. Editorial Acceptance (Pillar 3):** Sequential `eid` submission velocity (`sentForReview` Spearman $r = 0.9988$) fell `-56.3%`—tracking *eLife*'s published full-year drop (`6,396 -> 2,853`, `-55.4%`; Rodgers & Pattinson 2026; Behrens et al. 2026)—buffered by a `+4.63` pp jump in peer-review acceptance (`15.44% -> 20.06%`, HAC $t = +3.41$, Perm $p = 0.0002$, Sup-Wald $F = 53.14$) with invariant `v1` quality (`+0.54` pp, $p = 0.756$).
4. **Published-Output Synthetic Control & Policy-Label Groupings (Pillar 4):** Under strictly convex unique ADH Synthetic Control (`30` strict / `35` expanded clean journals), total published output fell `-0.552` log pts (Rank `1/30`, $p = 0.0333$; Expanded Rank `1/35`, $p = 0.0286$) and China-affiliated published output surged during Phase-1 PRC (`+4.86` pp share; Senior-PI `13.0% -> 20.5%`) before dropping `-1.091` log pts (`-66.4%`, Rank `1/30`, $p = 0.0333$; Expanded `-1.098`, Rank `1/35`, $p = 0.0286$; Senior-PI `9.1%` overnight). In a small-sample national policy-label comparison, `3` JIF-codified nations (`CN, ES, IT`: `-57.8%`) contracted `-21.2` pp deeper than `7` DORA-reformed nations (`-36.6%`, exact permutation $p = 0.0167$), tracking *eLife*'s published submission ledger (Spearman $\rho = 0.683$, $p = 0.0424$).
