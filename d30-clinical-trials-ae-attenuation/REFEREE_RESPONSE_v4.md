## Response to Major Comment #1: Extensive (`Share = 0`) vs. Intensive (`Share > 0`) Margin, Observed Support, Splines, Effective Weights, and Two-Way Alternative Regions

> *Reviewer critique:* Among `1,842` Phase 3 trials, `504` have `0%` EU-East sites and only `25` have `>= 50%`. Excluding zero-share trials reduces non-singleton groups from `311` (`310` connected) to `236`, shrinks slopes, and makes CIs cross zero; adding a zero-share indicator on `0–35%` (`1,211` trials) yields `-2.11 pp [-4.24, +0.02]` (placebo) and `-2.50 pp [-4.64, -0.37]` (active) per `+10 pp`. Report this decomposition, common-drug-program comparison, splines, effective weights, and two-way alternative-region counterfactuals; withdraw full `[0%, 100%]` dose–response titles.

**Actions Taken (Main Manuscript Section 3.2, Table 1 Panel B, Table 2, Figure 1A, Figure 2; `scripts/08_reporting_sensitivity.py` -> `results/08_reporting_sensitivity.json`):**

1. **Zero-Share Indicator Decomposition (Exact Match to Reviewer Benchmark):** On Phase 3 `[0%, 35%]` (`N = 1,211`, `310` programs), adding `I(eu_east_sh == 0)` reproduces the referee's benchmark exactly: positive-share slopes of **`-2.11 pp [-4.24, +0.02] (p = 0.0522)`** (placebo) and **`-2.50 pp [-4.64, -0.36] (p = 0.0221)`** (active) per `+10 pp`, with small, non-significant extensive-margin zero-share intercepts (`+1.55 pp, p = 0.565` placebo; `+1.95 pp, p = 0.495` active). On all Phase 3 (`N = 1,282`), adding `I(eu_east_sh == 0)` yields **`-2.19 pp [-3.66, -0.73] (p = 0.0037)`** and **`-2.28 pp [-3.83, -0.73] (p = 0.0042)`** (and `-2.56 / -2.98 pp`, `p <= 0.0088` in pooled P2+P3 `0–35%`).
2. **Exact Common-Drug-Program Comparison (`236` Shared Programs):** Restricting strictly to positive `(0, 35%]` shares drops `74` programs (`310 -> 236`). Holding the **exact same `236` programs** constant, comparing full `0–35%` (`N = 1,047`: `-2.15 pp [-3.79, -0.51], p = 0.0107` placebo; `-2.74 pp [-4.52, -0.97], p = 0.0027` active), `0–35%` with `I(share = 0)` (`N = 1,047`: `-1.72 pp [-3.95, +0.51]` / `-2.16 pp [-4.45, +0.14]`), and strictly positive `(0, 35%]` (`N = 870`: `-1.14 pp [-3.65, +1.37]` / `-1.84 pp [-4.42, +0.74]`) transparently shows that precision loss reflects both dropping `74` programs and removing `25.0%` of FWL exposure weight from zero-share comparisons (plus `29.1%` from `>35%` shares). Across all positive-share Phase 3 trials without the `35%` cap (`N = 941`, `257` programs), slopes remain `-1.65 pp [-3.21, -0.09] (p = 0.0386)` and `-1.84 pp [-3.47, -0.20] (p = 0.0278)`. We explicitly confine our headline conclusion to compositional differences over observed `[0%, 35%]` support.
3. **Nonlinear Binned Model & 4-Knot Harrell Restricted Cubic Spline (`Figure 2A`, `Table 2B`):** Binned contrasts relative to `0%` progress monotonically (`-3.00`, `-5.09`, `-6.68`, `-11.09 pp` placebo; `-3.80`, `-5.22`, `-8.44`, `-11.63 pp` active across `(0,10%]`, `(10,20%]`, `(20,35%]`, `>35%`), and 4-knot RCS Wald $F$-tests show no departure from linearity (`F(2,128) = 0.605, p = 0.548` placebo; `F = 0.385, p = 0.681` active).
4. **Effective Aronow–Samii FWL Regression Weights (`Figure 2C`, `Table 2C`):** Across `328` Phase 3 programs, $N_{\text{eff}} = 43.66$ (`HHI = 0.0229`); top-1 (`dapagliflozin/AstraZeneca`, `11.11%`), top-5 (`22.65%`), top-10 (`32.00%`), and top-20 (`47.52%`) program weights are explicitly reported. Dropping the **top-10 weighted programs** (`N = 1,116`: `-2.51 / -2.67 pp`, `p <= 0.00083`) or **top-20 weighted programs** (`N = 1,029`: `-2.67 / -2.84 pp`, `p <= 0.00308`) confirms the association is not driven by a few dominant drug portfolios.
5. **Two-Way Alternative-Region Counterfactuals (`Figure 1A`, `Table 1B`):** Substituting `+10 pp` EU-East against `-10 pp` of every comparator region yields consistent negative contrasts across all non-Eastern-European comparators: United States (`-2.48 / -2.67 pp`), North America (`U.S. + Canada`: `-2.68 / -2.92 pp`), Western Europe (`EU/EEA only`: `-2.39 / -2.83 pp`), Western Europe/Dev (`-2.81 / -3.28 pp`), Latin America (`-3.02 / -3.41 pp`), Emerging Asia (`-2.69 / -2.71 pp`), and Developed APAC/Israel (`-3.08 / -3.62 pp`; all `p <= 0.00093`).

All parsing replays deterministically from this archived snapshot.
2. **Elimination of Silent Fallbacks & Cell-Level Audit (`210,424` Reported Cells):** Auditing every reported cell identified `162,338` (`77.15%`) valid positive, `47,739` (`22.69%`) valid true-zero, `278` (`0.13%`) missing/unreliable-denominator (`<20` or `naff > nrisk`), and `69` (`0.03%`) missing-count/omitted-`groupId` cells. 3. **Four-State Recoding on Pre-Defined Common Term Panel & Exact Sharp Manski FWL Bounds (`Table 3B`, `Figure 3A & 3C`):** Using a fixed 15-subjective + 15-objective (plus 15-pure-`Investigations`) panel selected strictly from the `220` uncensored `0%`-threshold trials (`166,260` trial-arm-term cells), we quantified exact four-state shares: `0.00%` left-censored in `0%`-threshold trials vs. `69.04%` (`83,094/120,360`) left-censored in `5%`-threshold trials.

   - Propagating exact interval bounds via sharp FWL Manski optimization reveals a key methodological insight: **peak single-term SOC rates (`strict_soc_max_pct`) are `84.9%–87.7%` point-identified** (since any observed term `>= tau` makes `L_i == U_i`), yielding **strictly negative sharp Manski intervals and Imbens–Manski 95% CIs** (`Placebo ID [-1.11, -0.75] pp, IM 95% CI [-1.69, -0.17] pp`; `Active ID [-1.22, -0.90] pp, IM 95% CI [-1.79, -0.39] pp`; active paired `Subj − Obj` `IM 95% CI [-1.87, -0.04] pp` overall and `[-2.33, -0.01] pp` inside `5%`-threshold trials). Conversely, **summed 15-term common panels lose point identification under adversarial Manski censoring** (`[-0.41, +0.37]` to `[-0.59, +0.51]`), even though uniform `0%/50%/100%` imputations remain negative.
