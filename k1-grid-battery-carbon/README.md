### Headline Verified Findings (`v5`)
- **Centerpiece (Main Fig. 1 & Table 1) — Fleet-Wide AEF vs. CEMS-MEF Counterfactual Accounting Discrepancy (`49.77 TWh`, 14 Grids):** On identical metered storage dispatch without curtailment absorption (`α = 0`), consumption-weighted AEF implies **`-75.3 kg CO2/MWh (-3.75 Mt CO2)`** of abatement whereas CEMS-MEF estimates **`+74.2 kg CO2/MWh (+3.69 Mt CO2)`**—a **`+7.44 Mt CO2` counterfactual accounting discrepancy** reversing sign in `9/14` grids. Observed discharge is concentrated in `CISO + ERCO` (`78.5%` of discharge; `CISO` = `65.5%` of baseline discrepancy); Leave-One-BA-Out preserves sign reversal in `14/14` sub-fleets (`+2.57 [+2.30, +2.83] Mt` ex-`CISO`; `+0.93 [+0.80, +1.04] Mt` / `+87.1 kg/MWh` ex-`CISO & ERCO`, bounding aggregate Mt claims to this observed 14-market U.S. fleet and historical dispatch).
- **Unconditional Season-Stratified UTC-Day Block Bootstraps (`L = 7, 14, 28` d) & Table 1–2 Reconciliation Bridge (Main Fig. 2a & Table 2A):** Simultaneously re-estimating exact hourly AEF, 24h CEMS MEFs, unfixed discharge volumes, same-window bilateral trade weights (`2024-07..2026-09` & `2024-07..2025-12`), and CAISO curtailment across meteorological seasons (`DJF/MAM/JJA/SON`, `B = 1,000`) widens baseline 14d CIs by `1.85×` relative to conditional intervals (`+7.44 [+6.97, +7.98] Mt`, `7d [+7.02, +7.88]`, `28d [+6.90, +8.14]`). Under hybrid `15%` non-California absorption, fleet net CO2 spans zero (`+0.29 [-0.06, +0.57] Mt` full; `+0.16 [-0.03, +0.33] Mt` same-window), whereas the discrepancy vs. AEF stays strictly positive (`+4.03 [+3.62, +4.35] Mt` full, `28d [+3.66, +4.40]`; `+2.20 [+1.95, +2.44] Mt` same-window, `28d [+1.97, +2.52]`). The `0.0238 Mt` gap between Table 1 (`+7.4417 Mt`) and conditional v4 Table 2 (`+7.4179 Mt`) is bridged exactly (`+0.0019 Mt` sample/0.5 MW mask, `-0.0247 Mt` untrimmed `d_disp+d_imp` vs 8×-MAD `d_need`).
- **Out-of-Sample Temporal Holdout, 9-Quarter LQO CV, Lead-Lag (`k = -3..+3` h) & Measurement Cross-Checks (Main Fig. 2b & Supplementary Tables 4, 6):** Training 24h CEMS MEFs strictly on `2024-07..2025-09` predicts held-out `2025-10..2026-09` hourly `ΔCO2_CEMS` with pooled OOS `R² = 0.938`, discharge-weighted `R² = 0.761`, calibration slope `0.872` (`ERCO 0.967`, `MISO 0.945`, `CISO 0.759`, `AZPS 0.385`), and `3.5%` counterfactual discrepancy error (`+4.23` vs `+4.38 Mt`); 9-quarter LQO CV achieves mean `R² = 0.952` and `9/9` sign preservation (passing all 4 pre-specified criteria). Distributed `k = -3..+3` h lead-lag regressions concentrate `94.6%–96.1%` at contemporaneous `k = 0` in `CISO/ERCO/AZPS` (with small `≤2.4%` `k = -1` anticipation and `47.3%` contemporaneous share in small-fleet `MISO`, plus direct `CISO ΔImports/ΔBESS = -0.675` vs island `ERCO -0.022 MW/MW`), framing `-∂CO2/∂BESS`, pure CEMS stack slopes, and eGRID Non-Baseload `(1/η - 1)` transparently as measurement and order-of-magnitude cross-checks.
- **California Curtailment, Fixed-Profile Upper-Bound Scenarios & ERCOT Matched Cross-Regime Stress Test (Main Figs. 3–4):** Import-adjusted California net CO2 remains directionally undetermined across all block lengths and stratifications (`-0.80 [-14.20, +12.58]` main; `+1.34 [-21.86, +30.51]` observed `ΔLoad`; unconditional season-stratified `14d/28d`: `-0.66 [-12.47, +6.49]` / `[-11.67, +6.68]`). Fixed-profile BA-level upper-bound accounting scenarios (non-nodal; omitting LMPs & SOC constraints) cap incremental CAISO abatement at `0.0 / 4.3 / 8.0 / 15.2 GW` for `τ_local = 0% / 25% / 50% / 100%`. In ERCOT (framed as a cross-regime renewable-penetration stress test absent public EIA-930 interval curtailment), exact common-support `Season × Hour × Load-Quartile` matching (`120` cells) confirms coal enters the margin above `60%` renewables (`486.3 → 533.8 kg/MWh`, `+47.5 ± 12.9`; coal `206.9 → 290.2`, `+83.3 ± 16.2`; `Season × Hour × Load-Decile` FE `528.8`, coal `286.3`; midday solar `535.5`, coal `291.2`).
