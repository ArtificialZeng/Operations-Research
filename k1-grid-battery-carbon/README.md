## Overview

### Headline Verified Findings (`v6`)
- **Centerpiece (Main Fig. 1 & Table 1) — Fleet-Wide AEF vs. CEMS-MEF Counterfactual Accounting Discrepancy (`49.77 TWh`, 14 Grids):** On identical metered storage dispatch without curtailment absorption (`α = 0`), consumption-weighted AEF implies **`-75.3 kg CO2/MWh (-3.75 Mt CO2)`** of abatement whereas CEMS-MEF estimates **`+74.2 kg CO2/MWh (+3.69 Mt CO2)`**—a **`+7.44 Mt CO2` counterfactual accounting discrepancy** reversing sign in `9/14` grids. Observed discharge is concentrated in `CISO + ERCO` (`78.5%` of discharge; `CISO` = `65.5%` of baseline discrepancy); Leave-One-BA-Out preserves sign reversal in `14/14` sub-fleets (`+2.57 [+2.30, +2.83] Mt` ex-`CISO`; `+0.93 [+0.80, +1.04] Mt` / `+87.1 kg/MWh` ex-`CISO & ERCO`).
- **Unconditional Season-Stratified UTC-Day Block Bootstraps (`L = 7, 14, 28` d) & Table 1–2 Bridge (Main Fig. 2a & Table 2A):** Simultaneously re-estimating exact hourly AEF, 24h CEMS MEFs, unfixed discharge volumes, same-window bilateral trade weights (`2024-07..2026-09` & `2024-07..2025-12`), and CAISO curtailment across meteorological seasons (`DJF/MAM/JJA/SON`, `B = 1,000`) yields `+7.44 [+6.97, +7.98] Mt` (`14d`, `28d [+6.90, +8.14]`). Under hybrid `15%` non-California absorption, fleet net CO₂ spans zero (`+0.29 [-0.06, +0.57] Mt` full; `+0.16 [-0.03, +0.33] Mt` same-window), whereas the discrepancy vs. AEF stays strictly positive (`+4.03 [+3.62, +4.35] Mt` full; `+2.20 [+1.95, +2.44] Mt` same-window).
- **Out-of-Sample Temporal Holdout, 9-Quarter LQO CV, & Lead-Lag Diagnostics (Main Fig. 2b & Supplementary Table 6):** Training 24h CEMS MEFs strictly on `2024-07..2025-09` predicts held-out `2025-10..2026-09` hourly `ΔCO2_CEMS` with pooled OOS `R² = 0.938`, discharge-weighted `R² = 0.761`, calibration slope `0.872`, and `3.5%` discrepancy relative to the held-out model-based estimate (`+4.23` vs `+4.38 Mt`); 9-quarter LQO CV achieves mean `R² = 0.952` and `9/9` sign preservation (explicitly noting that OOS predictability of observed generator emissions does not equate to causal validation of a no-storage counterfactual without nodal SCED/OPF replay).
- **California Curtailment, Fixed-Profile Upper-Bound Scenarios & Direct 351-Unit ERCOT EPA CEMS Physical Mechanism (Main Figs. 3–4 & Supplementary Table 6):** Import-adjusted California net CO₂ remains directionally undetermined across all block lengths (`-0.80 [-14.20, +12.58]` main; `+1.34 [-21.86, +30.51]` observed `ΔLoad`). Fixed-profile BA-level upper-bound accounting scenarios cap incremental CAISO abatement at `0.0 / 4.3 / 8.0 / 15.2 GW` for `τ_local = 0% / 25% / 50% / 100%`. In ERCOT, exact `Season × Hour × Load-Quartile` matching (`120` cells) shows coal's estimated marginal contribution increases under high renewable penetration (`486.3 → 533.8 kg/MWh`, coal `206.9 → 290.2`, `+83.3 ± 16.2`); direct unit-level EPA CEMS telemetry across **`351` active ERCOT units (`6,965,982` unit-hours, `61` matched cells)** physically corroborates this mechanism: online `NGCT` gas peakers fall **`33.3 → 15.3` (`t = -9.1`)** and their share of fossil ramping `|ΔMW|` plunges **`25.1% → 7.3%` (`t = -10.2`)**, while committed coal exits its baseload ceiling (`>=95% HSL`: **`41.2% → 9.9%`**, `t = -15.1`), expands part-load operation (**`56.1% → 68.3%`**, `t = +5.9`) and active ramping (`30.8% → 36.5%`, `t = +3.2`), raising coal's share of total fossil ramping from **`22.1% → 30.0%` (`t = +7.0`)**.
