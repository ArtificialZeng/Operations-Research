## Overview
This archive contains the complete, reproducible physical-telemetry, generator-stack emissions, and econometric pipeline across three explicitly delineated empirical samples (**Box 1** in manuscript): (1) a `49`-BA diurnal gas-displacement panel (`2019–2026`), (2) a `14`-BA EPA CEMS carbon-accounting panel (`2024-07-01..2026-09-30`, `49.77 TWh` discharged) reconciling five EIA-930 telemetry discrepancies (including a 2-year `+5,553 MW` EIA-930 vs. CAISO/CEMS midday natural-gas reporting discrepancy), and (3) a CAISO non-circular curtailment subsample (`2024-07-01..2025-12-31`, `13,170` hrs, `4,347.2 GWh` official curtailment) with `B = 1,000` joint date-clustered block-bootstrap CIs. Includes `1,066/1,066` days of official CAISO standalone 5-minute `fuelsource.csv` telemetry, direct hourly EPA CEMS generator-stack `CO2` (`1,043,187` BA-hours, `99.94%` plant-to-BA CO2 match via EPA eGRID 2023 rev2), 5 publication figures (`300 DPI PNG` + vector `PDF`), 23-test regression suite, `REFEREE_RESPONSE_v2.md`, and 15-page camera-ready manuscript PDF.

### Headline Verified Findings (`v2`)
- **Pillar 1 — Telemetry Forensics & Discrepancy Reconciliation (`52,816.8 GWh` Full Telemetry Across 14 Grids):** Reconciles five EIA-930 telemetry discrepancies and anomalies: (i) a 2-year `+5,553 MW` mean (`+7,639 MW` peak) EIA-930 vs. CAISO/CEMS midday natural-gas reporting discrepancy (`2023-12..2025-12`, converging to `36.8 MW` in 2026 and lifting lag-0 EPA CEMS correlation from `r = 0.835` to `0.9962` upon splicing CAISO `fuelsource.csv`), (ii) `ERCO` (`7,224 GWh`) and `AZPS` (`5,690 GWh`) routing charging into `Unknown Energy Storage` (`RTE`: `1.49 → 0.778` and `∞ → 0.875`), (iii) standalone `CAISO` `fuelsource.csv` `RTE = 0.863` (vs. unphysical EIA-930 `1.076`; 13 clean AC-coupled grids fleet `RTE = 0.845`), (iv) filtering `FPL`'s `2,668 MW` outlier (`2025-08-27 HE 6`), and (v) verifying `0/4` pumped-hydro leakage. Exact 4-window diurnal taxonomy shows Southwest grids draw `71%–83%` midday solar (`0.9%–5.8%` overnight) while `MISO` (`56.7%`), `SOCO` (`56.4%`), and `DUK` (`50.6%`) draw over half of charging energy overnight (`23:00–06:00`).
- **Pillar 2 — Nationwide Demand-Weighted WLS Conditional Diurnal Gas Displacement (Sample 1: `G = 49, N = 4,589`):** Conditioning on solar and demand shifts, expanding BESS by `1%` of baseline demand is associated with a **`-0.3352`** reduction in the evening-minus-midday natural-gas spread (`SE = 0.0570, t_CR1 = -5.88, t_2way = -5.84, p_Rad = 0.004, p_Webb6 = 0.002, RI p = 0.0010`), contrasted transparently against the solar-confounded uncontrolled baseline (**`No Controls = -0.0579, SE = 0.0997, p_CR1 = 0.564, p_Rad = 0.576`**). Once conditioned, displacement strengthens outside California and Texas (`Drop CISO & ERCO, G = 47: -0.3810, t = -6.29, p_Rad = 0.002`), survives `Drop Top-7 BESS (G = 42: -0.3166)`, `BA × Post2024H2 FE (-0.3231, p_Rad = 0.001)`, 49-BA LOBAO (`∈ [-0.3651, -0.2923], max p = 0.0015`), peaks at Hour Ending 20 (`-0.2912, t = -8.95`), passes spotless distributed 6-month future lead tests (`F = 0.082, p = 0.921`), holds within high-frequency daily `BA × Year-Month FE` (`N = 139,497, t = -3.60`), confirms Proposition 1's Energy (`MWh`) vs. Power (`MW`) horse-race (`MWh p_WCB = 0.037` vs `MW p_WCB = 0.929`), and returns a precision zero on Nuclear (`-0.0005, p_Rad = 0.291`).
- **Pillar 3 — EPA CEMS Generator-Stack MEFs & Counterfactual Accounting Discrepancy (Sample 2: `49.77 TWh`, 14 Grids):** EPA CEMS directly measures hourly fossil generator smokestack emissions, whereas battery-induced net CO2 change relative to a no-storage baseline is estimated by our marginal displacement counterfactual model. Without curtailment absorption (`α = 0`), a naive consumption-weighted AEF counterfactual implies **`-75.3 kg CO2/MWh (-3.75 Mt CO2)`** of abatement, whereas our CEMS-MEF marginal displacement counterfactual (with exact `24×24` date-clustered covariances) estimates **`+74.2 kg CO2/MWh (+3.69 Mt CO2)`**—a **`+7.44 Mt CO2` counterfactual accounting discrepancy** (`9/14` grids flip sign), cross-validated against independent 24-hour EPA eGRID 2023 rev2 Peaker MEFs (`r = 0.977, MAD = 9.8 kg/MWh`).
- **Pillar 4 — Non-Circular Empirical CAISO Interior Curtailment Proof & Joint Block Bootstrap (Sample 3: `13,170` hrs):** Restricting strictly to consecutive interior curtailment regimes (`both t and t-1 in bin`), real curtailment above `2,000 MW` slashes California's **in-state domestic** CEMS MEF by **83%** (`+160.6 → +26.8 kg/MWh`; pure exogenous `ΔLoad` domestic **83%**, `+164.4 → +27.9`) and **system-wide (import-adjusted)** CEMS MEF by **60%** (`+268.8 → +107.0`; pure `ΔLoad` system **62%**, `+187.5 → +70.5`), while marginal un-curtailment jumps from exact **`0.0%`** to **`62.4%`**. Propagating diurnal, cross-BA import (`10` neighbor BAs), curtailment-regime, and hourly cycling-weight uncertainty jointly via a **`B = 1,000` date-clustered block bootstrap** places CAISO curtailment-stratified system-wide net CO2 at **`-0.80 kg/MWh` (`boot SE = 4.76`, 95% CI `[-10.39, +8.15]`, `p = 0.810`)** under our main specification and **`+1.34 kg/MWh` (`boot SE = 8.26`, 95% CI `[-14.14, +18.62]`, `p = 0.826`)** under the pure exogenous `ΔLoad` alternative (domestic-only: **`-30.64 kg/MWh`**, 95% CI `[-39.30, -22.47]`; pure `ΔLoad` domestic: **`-30.67 kg/MWh`**, 95% CI `[-44.07, -17.04]`). Both system-wide specifications sit statistically indistinguishable from zero (near carbon neutrality), erasing `>99%` of naive AEF's `-104.7 kg/MWh` claim.
