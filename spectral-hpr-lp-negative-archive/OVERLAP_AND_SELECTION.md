### Spectral-Startup Equivalence Check
- **Equivalent to existing art (zero novelty claimed):** Initializing $\lambda_A \approx \|A\|_2^2$ via power iteration (`cuPDLPx` / `HPR-LP`), adapting scalar $\eta_k$ via local step ratios (`PDLP` v2 `arXiv:2501.07018`, Xu & He `arXiv:2603.00644`), monitoring scalar spectral radius (Fercoq `doi:10.1093/imanum/drag084`), or recomputing scalar $\|A_{B,F}\|_2$ via masked power iteration (`cuPDLPx` ASB). All reduce to scalar multiples of $I_m$ in the dual update.
- **Structurally inequivalent mechanism tested:** Non-diagonal rank-$r$ dual semi-proximal operator $\mathcal{M}_y^{(r,\text{cert})} = \tilde{\theta}^2 I_m + \hat{U}_r \operatorname{diag}(\tilde{\mu}_i - \tilde{\theta}^2) \hat{U}_r^\top$, which applies distinct inverse curvatures $1/\tilde{\mu}_i$ on $\operatorname{span}(\hat{U}_r)$ and $1/\tilde{\theta}^2 \gg 1/\tilde{\mu}_1$ on $\hat{U}_r^\perp$ simultaneously at every sweep.

**Overlap Verdict:** A genuinely distinct, mathematically sharp mechanism survives full overlap scrutiny: **non-diagonal, matrix-free, certified rank-$r$ spectral semi-proximal operator preconditioning $\mathcal{T}_1^{(r)}$ inside standard-form HPR-LP**, active from iteration 0 and remaining effective even when 100% of constraints are binding equalities (where `cuPDLPx` ASB cannot exclude rows and all static/online diagonal scalings hit an exact lower bound).

## 3. Exact Mathematical Mechanism & Theoretical Statements

Consider a linear program in standard equality form (any general LP $l_c \le Gx \le u_c, \; l_v \le x \le u_v$ converts exactly by introducing box-bounded slacks $s_i \in [l_{c,i}, u_{c,i}]$ only on non-equality rows $l_{c,i} < u_{c,i}$, yielding $A = [G_{\mathcal{E}}, 0; G_{\mathcal{I}}, -I]$ and $\mathcal{K}=\{b\}$):
$$\min_{x \in \mathcal{C}} \langle c, x \rangle \quad \text{s.t.} \quad A x = b, \qquad \mathcal{C} = [l_v, u_v].$$

In Algorithm 1 of `HPR-LP` (`arXiv:2611.01872`), the dual subproblem uses any self-adjoint semi-proximal operator $\mathcal{T}_1 \succeq 0$ with $\mathcal{M}_y := A A^* + \mathcal{T}_1 \succ 0$. Because $\mathcal{K} = \{b\}$ makes $\delta_{\mathcal{K}}^*(-y) = -\langle b, y \rangle$ linear, substituting $\sigma(\bar{z}^{k+1}-c) = \bar{x}^{k+1} - x^k - \sigma A^* y^k$ yields the **exact matrix-free dual sweep**:
$$\bar{y}^{k+1} = y^k + \frac{1}{\sigma} \mathcal{M}_y^{-1}\!\left(b - A(2\bar{x}^{k+1} - x^k)\right).$$

### Theorem 1 (Strict Loewner & HPR-Metric Dominance)
Let $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ have singular values $s_1 \ge \dots \ge s_m \ge 0$ and orthonormal left singular vectors $u_1, \dots, u_m$. For $0 \le r < m$ with $s_{r+1} > 0$, define
$$\mathcal{M}_y^{(r)} := s_{r+1}^2 I_m + \sum_{i=1}^r (s_i^2 - s_{r+1}^2) u_i u_i^\top, \qquad \mathcal{T}_1^{(r)} := \mathcal{M}_y^{(r)} - A A^*.$$
Then:
1. $0 \preceq \mathcal{T}_1^{(r)} \preceq \mathcal{T}_1^{(r-1)} \preceq \dots \preceq \mathcal{T}_1^{(0)} = s_1^2 I_m - A A^*$, with $\mathcal{T}_1^{(r)} u_i = 0$ for all $i \le r$ (exact augmented-Lagrangian step on the top-$r$ singular subspace) and $\mathcal{M}_y^{(r)} \preceq s_1^2 I_m$.
2. In Theorem 2 of `HPR-LP` (`arXiv:2611.01872`, eqs. 8 & 10a–10c), for **every** initialization $w^0$, KKT solution $w^*$, and penalty $\sigma > 0$, both the anchor distance $R_0 = \|w^0 - w^*\|_{\mathcal{M}_\sigma^{(r)}}$ and the KKT residual operator norm $\|\sqrt{\mathcal{T}_1^{(r)}}\|_2 = \sqrt{s_{r+1}^2 - s_m^2}$ are **monotonically non-increasing in $r$** and strictly smaller than the scalar baseline ($r=0$) whenever $s_1 > s_{r+1}$ and $\Delta y \notin \operatorname{span}(u_1)$.
3. Applying $(\mathcal{M}_y^{(r)})^{-1} v = s_{r+1}^{-2}\!\left(v - \sum_{i=1}^r (1 - s_{r+1}^2/s_i^2)(u_i^\top v) u_i\right)$ and evaluating the exact restart/penalty metric $\|v\|_{\mathcal{M}_y^{(r)}}^2$ each cost **$2mr$ FLOPs and zero extra SpMVs**.

### Theorem 2 (Diagonal-Preconditioning Impossibility vs. Rank-1 Collapse)
Let $M_\rho = (1 - \rho) I_m + \rho \mathbf{1}_m \mathbf{1}_m^\top$ with $m \ge 2$ and $\rho \in (0, 1)$. For **every** pair of positive diagonal matrices $D_r = \operatorname{diag}(r_1,\dots,r_m) \succ 0$ and $D_c = \operatorname{diag}(c_1,\dots,c_m) \succ 0$ (covering static Ruiz, Curtis–Reid, Extended RMS, Pock–Chambolle, and any iterate of online diagonal preconditioning `arXiv:2506.17650`):
$$\kappa_2(D_r M_\rho D_c) := \frac{s_1(D_r M_\rho D_c)}{s_m(D_r M_\rho D_c)} > \frac{(m - 1)\rho}{1 - \rho} = \Omega(m).$$
*(Proof sketch: let $a_i = r_i c_i$, sorted $0 < a_{(1)} \le a_{(2)} \le \dots \le a_{(m)}$. Testing $u = r/\|r\|_2$, $v = c/\|c\|_2$ and Cauchy–Schwarz $\|r\|_2\|c\|_2 \ge \sum_i a_i > (m-1)a_{(2)}$ yields $s_1(D_r M_\rho D_c) > (m-1)\rho\,a_{(2)}$. Testing unit $v$ supported on indices $(1),(2)$ with $c_{(1)}v_{(1)} + c_{(2)}v_{(2)} = 0$ yields $M_\rho D_c v = (1-\rho) D_c v$ and hence $s_m(D_r M_\rho D_c) \le (1-\rho) a_{(2)}$. Dividing gives the bound.)*  
In contrast, the rank-1 spectral operator $\mathcal{M}_y^{(1)}$ reduces the effective dual-scaled spectrum of $(\mathcal{M}_y^{(1)})^{-1/2} M_\rho$ to **condition number $1$** ($\mathcal{T}_1^{(1)} = 0$ identically!). More generally, for rectangular standard-form constraints $A = [M_\rho \;\; \gamma I_m]$ or $A A^\top = (1-\rho)I_m + \rho U_k \Lambda_k U_k^\top$, **every** left diagonal scaling $D_r \succ 0$ satisfies $\kappa_2(D_r A) \ge \sqrt{(m-1)\rho/(1-\rho)} = \Omega(\sqrt{m})$ while rank-$k$ $\mathcal{M}_y^{(k)}$ achieves exact $\mathcal{T}_1^{(k)} = 0$ in $O(mk)$ FLOPs/iter.

### Theorem 3 (Exact Schur-Complement PSD Certification for Inexact Subspaces)
Given **any** orthonormal matrix $\hat{U}_r \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times r}$ (e.g., from cheap $q$-pass randomized SVD or Lanczos), let $V_1 = A^\top \hat{U}_r$, $H = V_1^\top V_1 = \hat{U}_r^\top A A^\top \hat{U}_r \in \mathbb{S}_+^r$, and compute the exact block residual $R = A V_1 - \hat{U}_r H = (I_m - \hat{U}_r \hat{U}_r^\top) A A^\top \hat{U}_r \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times r}$ (total: $2r$ SpMVs). Let $H = Q \operatorname{diag}(\mu_1, \dots, \mu_r) Q^\top$ (replacing $\hat{U}_r \leftarrow \hat{U}_r Q$, $R \leftarrow R Q$), and let $\theta_\perp^2$ upper-bound $\|(I_m - \hat{U}_r \hat{U}_r^\top) A\|_2^2$ (deflated power iteration with safety factor $1.02$). Define $\beta := \|R \operatorname{diag}(\mu_i^{-1/2})\|_2$ (over $\mu_i > 0$). Then for any $\epsilon > 0$:
$$\mathcal{M}_y^{(r,\text{cert})} := \tilde{\theta}^2 I_m + \hat{U}_r \operatorname{diag}\!\left(\max((1+\epsilon)\mu_i, \tilde{\theta}^2) - \tilde{\theta}^2\right) \hat{U}_r^\top, \qquad \tilde{\theta}^2 := \theta_\perp^2 + \frac{\beta^2}{\epsilon}$$
satisfies $\mathcal{M}_y^{(r,\text{cert})} \succeq A A^*$ identically ($\mathcal{T}_1^{(r,\text{cert})} \succeq 0$ with zero exact-SVD assumption!). Choosing $\epsilon^* = \beta / \theta_\perp$ (when $\beta > 0$) gives a first-order perturbation $\tilde{\theta}^2 = \theta_\perp^2 + \beta \theta_\perp$ while keeping $\mathcal{M}_y^{(r,\text{cert})}$ strictly diagonal in the Ritz basis $\hat{U}_r$, preserving the $2mr$-FLOP Woodbury inverse.
